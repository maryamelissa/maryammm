📘 About Focus Zone

Focus Zone is a simple educational and productivity website designed to help students and users organize their time and improve their daily life.

The main goal of this project is to help people stay focused, manage their studies, and understand important life topics such as social life, politics, technology, motivation, and health.

This website contains different sections: - Study Plan: helps you organize your learning schedule - Social Life: improves communication skills - Politics: increases awareness about society - Motivation: keeps you strong and focused - Technology: explains modern digital world - Health: reminds you to take care of yourself

The website is built using only HTML and CSS, with animations and a modern design to make the experience simple and attractive for users.

Focus Zone is made for students who want to improve themselves step by step and build better habits for the future.
